Audio-Driven Lip Sync on a Video Diffusion Foundation Model

Mask-free lip synchronization built on WAN2.2 — a large-scale video generation foundation model.

Overview

  • Mask-free lip sync on WAN2.2 video diffusion model
  • Gaussian-weighted spatial CFG for localized mouth control
  • Multi-stage curriculum: high-noise → structure, low-noise → detail
  • 50 → 4 step inference via DMD distillation
  • VL-assisted supervision for temporal & semantic consistency
  • Generalizes to children's animation domain
