I was a Senior Algorithm Engineer at Alibaba in Beijing until May 2026, where I worked on controllable visual generation, particularly precise editing under identity- and content-preservation constraints.

My current interests center on how video models interpret multimodal inputs and capture the relationships among language, visual content, motion, and temporal structure, enabling more controllable video generation and action prediction.

I earned my master's degree from Northeastern University (China) in 2019 under the supervision of Professor Lixin Tang. I received my bachelor's degree from the same university in 2016.
